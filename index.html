The current state

Most small businesses run on spreadsheets, paper and group texts.

It works until it doesn't. Hours go missing. Jobs fall through the cracks. A bid lives in someone's inbox. Off-the-shelf software is built for everyone, so it fits no one quite right. Narro builds the one tool you actually need, shaped around how your team already works.

Spreadsheets

Versions everywhere, formulas nobody trusts, and one person who knows how it all works.

Paper

Timecards and job sheets that get lost, retyped, or show up a week late.

Group texts

Schedules and approvals buried under a hundred messages.

Tool templates

Small tools. Built for one job each.

Every tool starts from a proven template and is tailored to your fields, your roles and your process. Start with one. Add more as you grow.

Questions

How long does a tool take to build?

Most first tools are ready in a few weeks, since we start from a template rather than from scratch. We'll give you a clear timeline after the workflow call.

What does it cost?

It depends on the tool. You'll get a fixed quote before any work starts, then a simple monthly fee that covers hosting, backups and updates. [Add starting price]

Does my team need to install anything?

No. Your team opens yourcompany.narro.cloud in a browser on their phone or computer and signs in. That's it.

Who owns our data?

You do. Each company's data is kept separate, and you can export it whenever you want.

Can we change it later?

Yes. Your business will change, and your tool should too. Ask for a new field, report or workflow and we'll update it.
